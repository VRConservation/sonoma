## Poverty
California's economy is the 5th largest in the world (if it was a country) and so often is not mentioned in the same sentence with poverty. However, pockets of the state have high levels of poverty (Figure 1).  These are located in urban areas, the northern and southern ends of the state and the southern San Joaquin Valley.

In Sonoma County, most economically disadvantaged citizens live in Santa Rosa or Windsor's highly dense urban corridor. They are dispersed throughout more rural parts of the county. Overall, Sonoma County has a 9% poverty rate and generally lower percentages of economically disadvantaged citizens than other parts of California (Figure 2).
